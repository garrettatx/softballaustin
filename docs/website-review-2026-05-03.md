# Softball Austin Website Review

Date: 2026-05-03
Site: https://www.softballaustin.org/
Platform notes: WordPress, Kadence theme, Kadence Blocks

## Executive Summary

Softball Austin has the right foundation: clear community positioning, current Spring 2026 schedule links, strong trust signals, and a useful navigation structure for players, managers, and visitors. The biggest immediate issue is content freshness. Several prominent pages still reference Spring 2025 or 2025 tournament information, which can reduce trust for new players, managers, sponsors, and search engines.

The first review pass should focus on:

1. Updating time-sensitive content across homepage, manager, FAQ, registration, and tournament sections.
2. Tightening the main conversion paths for new players, returning players, managers, sponsors, and volunteers.
3. Improving local SEO around LGBTQ+ softball in Austin, recreational softball, women's softball, and inclusive sports.
4. Preserving Kadence block structure when editing pages in WordPress.

## Early Findings

### 1. Homepage Has Current News But Old Tournament Content

The homepage shows current Spring 2026 news items, including "Spring 2026 Schedule" and "Spring 2026 Info." However, the Texas Hoedown promotion still references May 2025 registration dates and deadlines.

Impact: Visitors may not know whether tournament information is current, and search engines may treat the page as partially stale.

Recommended action: Replace or archive expired 2025 tournament promotion content. If 2026 dates are not ready, use a short "2026 details coming soon" module with an email or LeagueApps CTA.

Source: https://www.softballaustin.org/

### 2. Manager's Guide Is Still Spring 2025

The Manager's Guide is useful, but it leads with Spring 2025 deadlines, dates, forms, and manager meeting details.

Impact: This is likely a high-intent page for team managers. Outdated dates can create operational confusion and unnecessary board emails.

Recommended action: Update for the current season or add a prominent archive/current-season distinction.

Source: https://www.softballaustin.org/info/managers-guide/

### 3. FAQ Has Useful Content But Some Details Need Refreshing

The FAQ answers core player questions well, including how to play, equipment, location, schedule, rules, NAGAAA/iPride, ASANA, and sponsors. Some examples appear dated, including Spring 2023 fee references.

Impact: The FAQ can rank and convert well, but dated fee examples weaken confidence.

Recommended action: Replace old exact fee references with current ranges, current-season links, or "fees are announced before each season" language.

Source: https://www.softballaustin.org/info/faqs/

### 4. Schedule Page Is Clear But Could Be a Stronger Hub

The Schedule + Standings page has Spring 2026 schedule links and LeagueApps division links.

Impact: This is probably one of the highest-traffic utility pages during the season. It should be treated as a core hub, not just a link list.

Recommended action: Add brief context for game days, fields, rainout status, and registration/standings expectations. Keep it concise so returning players can scan quickly.

Source: https://www.softballaustin.org/schedule/

### 5. Local SEO Opportunity Is Underdeveloped

The site clearly establishes Softball Austin as an LGBTQ+ softball league in Austin, but there is room to strengthen local discovery for related searches:

- LGBTQ+ softball league Austin
- gay softball Austin
- queer softball Austin
- recreational softball league Austin
- women's softball Austin
- inclusive sports Austin

Impact: More complete topical coverage can help new players, sponsors, and community partners find the league.

Recommended action: Build or improve a few durable landing/info pages instead of relying only on seasonal posts.
